Why MealOps

MealOps handles the complexity agents shouldn't have to.

Every POS, marketplace, and ordering channel works differently. MealOps turns them into one consistent way to find, price, order, and track food.

One connection, every channel

POS systems, marketplaces, and direct ordering each have their own APIs and rules. Agents connect once.

Live menus and availability

Menus, modifiers, prices, store hours, and sold-out items, kept current across every source.

Timing and fulfillment

Prep and delivery lead times, scheduled orders, and each restaurant's local time zone, checked before an order is placed.

Quotes and payments

Totals quoted up front, with payment authorized and captured against the confirmed amount.

Status and tracking

Confirmations, order status, and delivery tracking flow back to the agent automatically.

Trusted access

Only approved agents can place orders, and each restaurant's rules are enforced server-side.

Phantom

The infrastructure powering the network.

Phantom is the ordering layer underneath MealOps. Agents connect to a single MCP endpoint to discover restaurants, read live menus, get quotes, pay, place orders, and track them.
