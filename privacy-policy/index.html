MealOps Privacy Policy

How MealOps Protects Your Information

Effective date: October 6, 2026

MealOps, Inc. (“MealOps”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates a restaurant network that connects teams, organizations, and the AI agents acting for them to restaurants across point-of-sale systems, marketplaces, and direct ordering channels, including through the CoachImHungry app and the Phantom platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard information when you interact with our websites, applications, communications, and related services (collectively, the “Services”). It also outlines the choices you have about your information and how to reach us with questions.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us, information created when you use the Services, and information partners send us to fulfill your orders.

  • Account and profile. Name, email address, password (managed by our authentication provider), phone number, role, organization and team, and communication preferences.
  • Team and roster information. Information you or your team’s admins add about team members, such as names, contact details, roles, birthdays, and dietary and allergy information.
  • Orders. The restaurant, items, who each item is for, dietary notes and special instructions, pickup or delivery time, delivery address (including map coordinates), on-site contact name and phone number, delivery instructions, receipts, and order status.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive limited details such as card brand, last four digits, expiration date, and transaction records. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Location. Addresses you enter, with suggestions from Google Maps. We collect your device’s precise location only if you choose to use your current location.
  • SMS messaging data. Web form consent submissions (checkbox selections, phone numbers, timestamps, and device or IP metadata), opt-in and opt-out timestamps, delivery receipts, and limited message content needed to provide transactional meal updates.
  • Messages to our AI assistant. What you ask the CoachImHungry assistant and the responses it gives.
  • Information from AI agents and apps acting for you. If you order through an AI agent or app built by another company that connects to MealOps, that company sends us what is needed for your order, such as your name, contact details, delivery address, team, dietary notes, and order details.
  • Communications. Emails and messages you send us, including through our website contact form.
  • Device and usage data. IP address, browser and device type, pages and features used, and server logs, plus information stored on your device to keep you signed in and remember your preferences.

2. How We Use Information

  • Providing the Services and your orders. Sending orders to restaurants and marketplaces, processing payments, coordinating pickup and delivery, and issuing receipts, refunds, and order updates.
  • Team coordination. Shared carts, meal planning, rosters, and permissions for your team.
  • AI features. Answering your requests, suggesting meals, and building carts, as described in Section 4.
  • Communicating with you. Responding to inquiries, sending service and order updates, and telling you about new features where the law allows.
  • SMS notifications. Prompting team members in a shared cart to set SMS preferences, sending transactional texts once consent is recorded, honoring opt-in and opt-out requests, and meeting carrier and legal requirements.
  • Safety and integrity. Preventing fraud and abuse, securing accounts, responding to payment disputes, enforcing our terms, and meeting legal obligations.
  • Improving the Services. Troubleshooting and analyzing usage, including with aggregated or de-identified information.

3. When We Share Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only as described below.

  • Restaurants. To fulfill an order, the restaurant receives the order details, names on items, dietary notes, pickup or delivery time, contact name, phone number, and email, and the delivery address. This may pass through the point-of-sale or ordering provider that connects the restaurant to us, such as Deliverect. Because restaurants are the sellers of record for restaurant orders, we also share the transaction records they need for accounting and tax reporting, such as amounts and taxes collected on their behalf.
  • Marketplaces and couriers. For marketplace orders, MealOps places the order with the marketplace (currently DoorDash) from a MealOps account and shares the order contents, delivery address and instructions, requested time, and the name and contact details the marketplace and its couriers need to complete the order. The marketplace does not receive your payment details from us and handles this information under its own privacy policy. Courier partners, such as DeliverThat, receive the recipient’s name, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Your team. Admins, coaches, and teammates can see team orders, shared carts, and roster information according to their role.
  • Service providers. Companies that help us run the Services, under contracts that limit how they use the information: hosting and databases (Supabase, Railway, Netlify), payments (Stripe), text messages (Twilio), email (Resend), our AI assistant (Anthropic), maps and address search (Google Maps Platform), and network and website analytics services (Cloudflare).
  • Text message carriers. Messaging providers and carriers that deliver our texts, so they can route messages, honor opt-outs, and monitor for fraud.
  • Tax authorities. Where MealOps must report or remit taxes on orders it facilitates, the transaction information the law requires.
  • AI agents and apps you use. If an agent or app built by another company orders for you, we send it your order’s quotes, status, and tracking. That company’s own privacy policy governs how it uses that information.
  • Payment disputes. If a charge is disputed, we may share order records, receipts, and delivery details with our payment processor and the card networks.
  • Legal and safety reasons. When required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MealOps, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers. As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

4. AI Features and Agents

  • Our assistant. When you use the CoachImHungry assistant, we send your messages and the team and order information needed to answer them, which can include roster and dietary information, to an AI model provider (currently Anthropic). The provider processes it to generate responses for us under terms that limit its use of that information.
  • Agents that order for you. AI agents, including our own, can search restaurants, build carts, and place orders within the permissions you give them. Orders they place are recorded in your order history.
  • Check important details. AI output can be wrong. Do not rely on it for allergy or medical decisions; confirm allergen information with the restaurant.

5. Dietary and Allergy Information

Dietary and allergy information can be health-related information under some laws. We use it only to fulfill orders and coordinate team meals, share it only with the people and partners who prepare and deliver those orders, and never use it for advertising. You or your team’s admins can update or remove it at any time.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and browser storage that are needed to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the CoachImHungry app. Our website, mealops.ai, uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits; it does not set cookies or track you across other sites, and it records information such as the page you viewed, the referring page, your browser type, and your country. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

7. Data Retention and Security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Order and payment records are kept as long as accounting, tax, and other laws require. SMS consent and delivery logs are kept as long as needed to demonstrate compliance with messaging rules. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your information, except records we must keep. We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information, but no system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep your credentials private.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Account settings. Update your profile, notification preferences, and team information in the Services.
  • Marketing emails. Unsubscribe using the link in any promotional email, or email support@mealops.ai.
  • SMS preferences. Use the shared cart web form to update consent, reply STOP to cancel MealOps texts, START to resume, or HELP for help, or email support@mealops.ai.
  • Privacy rights. Depending on where you live, including California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, get a copy of it, correct it, and delete it. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising, and use sensitive information only to provide the Services, there is nothing to opt out of on those fronts.
  • How to make a request. Email support@mealops.ai from the address on your account. We may need to verify your identity, and you may use an authorized agent. If we decline your request, you can ask us to reconsider by replying to our decision. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

9. International Data Transfers

MealOps operates in the United States and may process information in the U.S. or other countries where our service providers are located. When we transfer information, we use appropriate safeguards, such as contractual protections, to meet applicable legal requirements.

10. Children and Teens

The Services are meant to be used by adults, such as coaches, staff, and organizers, and are not directed to children under 13. Team staff may add information about team members who are under 18, such as high school athletes, to coordinate team meals. We use that information only for that purpose, never sell it or use it for advertising, and rely on the team to have any consent the law requires, including parental consent for any child under 13. A parent or guardian can ask us to review or delete a minor’s information by emailing support@mealops.ai. If we learn that we collected information from a child under 13 without the required consent, we will delete it.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will post a notice on our website or notify you through the Services. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised.
